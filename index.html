Understanding UAE End of Service Benefits

End of service gratuity is a financial benefit paid to eligible employees after completing qualifying service under UAE labour law. The amount depends on salary, contract type and length of employment.

This educational resource explains the main concepts behind gratuity calculations and helps employees understand how the rules generally work before discussing their final settlement with their employer.
